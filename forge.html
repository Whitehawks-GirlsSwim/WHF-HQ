THE STRENGTH PROTOCOL

Earn your strength.

Four-session upper/lower program. Complete each exercise with good form and leave roughly two reps in reserve. Build steadily, not recklessly.

THE BUILD-UP

Weeks 1–2: start with 2 sets, 2–3 nonconsecutive sessions. Weeks 3–4: build toward 3 sessions. From week 5, work toward the 4-session split if recovery allows. Use the lighter option whenever needed.

ON WEGOVY: Protein plus progressive lifting can help reduce muscle loss during weight reduction. Smaller meals may be easier when appetite is low. Eat enough to recover, drink regularly, and don't force a hard workout when feeling unwell. Persistent vomiting or trouble keeping fluids down warrants prompt medical advice; severe persistent abdominal pain warrants urgent evaluation.
