Earn your strength.

Four-session upper/lower program. Complete each exercise with good form and leave roughly two reps in reserve. Build steadily, not recklessly.

THE BUILD-UP

Weeks 1–2: start with 2 sets, 2–3 nonconsecutive sessions. Weeks 3–4: build toward 3 sessions. From week 5, work toward the 4-session split if recovery allows. Use the lighter option whenever needed.
